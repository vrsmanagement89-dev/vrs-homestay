Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What are the check-in and check-out timings?
VRS Homestay operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Guests can check in or check out at any time — we are always here to welcome you.
How close is VRS Homestay to the Mayuranathar Temple?
VRS Homestay is just 100 metres from the famous Mayuranathar Temple — one of Tamil Nadu's most revered Shiva temples. You can walk there easily from our property.
Is the Clock Tower landmark nearby?
Yes! Our homestay is located right next to the Clock Tower — the main landmark of Mayiladuthurai. It makes our location very easy to find and perfectly central to everything in town.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 48 hours before check-in are fully refundable. After that, one night's charge may apply. Please contact us for special circumstances and we will do our best to accommodate you.
Can we book multiple houses for a group stay?
Absolutely! We have six houses available and can accommodate group bookings. Please contact us directly for group rates, availability and any special arrangements you may need.
Are hospitals and medical facilities nearby?
Yes, several leading hospitals and medical facilities are located close to our property. Our central location ensures that healthcare, shopping and all essential services are always within easy reach.
Do you offer airport transfers?
Yes, airport and railway station transfers can be arranged on request. Please inform us of your travel details at least 24 hours in advance and we will coordinate a comfortable pickup for you.
