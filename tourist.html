Arulmigu Mayuranathar Temple

One of Tamil Nadu's most revered Shiva temples, the Mayuranathar Temple is the very soul of Mayiladuthurai. According to Hindu mythology, Goddess Parvathi worshipped Lord Shiva in the form of a peacock (Mayura) here — giving the town its name. The temple's majestic Dravidian gopuram and intricate carvings are a testament to centuries of Chola architectural mastery.

This sacred site is also part of the Navagraha pilgrimage circuit, making it a starting point for thousands of devotees every year. VRS Homestay is just 100 metres away — making it the most convenient base for your temple visit.

Vaitheeswaran Koil

Dedicated to Lord Shiva as the divine physician, Vaitheeswaran Koil is believed to grant healing and relief from illness. It represents Mars (Chevvai) in the Navagraha circuit and draws thousands of pilgrims seeking cure and blessings every day.

Thirunallaru Saniswaran Temple

One of the very few temples in India dedicated solely to Lord Shani (Saturn). Named after King Nala Maharaja who was freed from Saturn's malefic influence here. A sacred dip in the Nala Theertham is believed to wash away all bad karma.

Thirunageswaram (Rahu Temple)

Dedicated to Lord Rahu, this temple near Kumbakonam is famous for a miraculous daily ritual — milk poured over the idol turns blue as it passes and returns white as it reaches the floor. One of the most visited Navagraha temples in South India.

Poompuhar Beach

Located at the mouth of the sacred Cauvery River where it meets the Bay of Bengal, Poompuhar Beach offers stunning sunrise and sunset views. This ancient port city is described in the Tamil epic Silappathikaram and is over 2000 years old.

Tharangambadi (Tranquebar)

A historic Danish colonial settlement on the Coromandel Coast. Fort Dansborg, built in 1620, is a beautifully preserved colonial fort now under the Tamil Nadu Archaeological Department. The iconic Kings Street, churches and the beachside fort make it a photographer's paradise.

Sirkazhi Sattanathar Temple

A magnificent Dravidian temple glorified in the sacred Thevaram hymns. This is the birthplace of the great Saiva saint Thirugnana Sambandar, who received divine grace from Lord Shiva and Parvathi here. The annual Thirumulaippal festival in Chithirai is celebrated grandly.

Gangaikonda Cholapuram

Built by the great Chola emperor Rajendra Chola I in the 11th century, this UNESCO-listed Brihadeeswarar Temple rival Thanjavur in grandeur. The towering vimana and its extraordinary sculpture make it one of the finest examples of Chola architecture in existence.

Thiruvidaimarudur Temple

Known as the Mahalingeswarar Temple, this ancient Shiva shrine represents Earth (Bhumi) in the Navagraha circuit. One of the Paadal Petra Sthalams glorified by the Thevaram saints, it features remarkable Chola-era architecture and a grand sacred tank.

Alangudi (Jupiter Temple)

Dedicated to Lord Guru (Jupiter), the Apatsahayeswarar Temple at Alangudi is one of the most important Navagraha temples. Visiting this temple is believed to remove obstacles, bring wisdom and bestow prosperity. The presiding deity Brahaspati blesses all devotees who seek guidance.
