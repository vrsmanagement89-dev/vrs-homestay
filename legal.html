Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

VRS Homestay is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and how we keep it safe.

What We Collect

  • Name, phone number and email address submitted via our booking form
  • Check-in and check-out dates and guest count for reservation purposes
  • Special requests or messages you submit voluntarily

How We Use It

  • To process and confirm your reservation at VRS Homestay
  • To contact you regarding your booking via phone, WhatsApp or email
  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with any third party

Data Security

All form submissions are handled securely through Netlify Forms. We do not store payment information on our website. Your data is used solely for booking and communication purposes.

Contact

Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

We understand that plans can change. Our refund policy is designed to be fair and transparent for all guests.

Cancellation & Refunds

  • Cancellations made 48 hours or more before check-in — full refund
  • Cancellations made less than 48 hours before check-in — one night's charge applies
  • No-show without prior notice — full booking amount is non-refundable

How to Cancel

  • Call or WhatsApp us directly at +91 9363004340
  • Email us at vrsmanagement89@gmail.com with your booking details
  • Cancellation is confirmed only after acknowledgement from our team

Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days
  • Refunds are returned via the same payment method used at booking
  • Bank processing time may vary depending on your bank

Special Circumstances

In case of medical emergencies or natural disasters, please contact us directly. We will review such cases individually and do our best to accommodate a fair resolution.
