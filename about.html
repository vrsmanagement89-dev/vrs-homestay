Our Story: A Vision of Elegant Comfort

Located in the heart of Mayiladuthurai, VRS Homestay was created to provide a peaceful and luxurious stay experience for travelers visiting this historic town.

Just 100 meters from the sacred Mayuranathar Temple and near the iconic Clock Tower, our homestay places guests at the center of culture, tradition, and convenience.

A Message from the Founder

Our family has been part of the hospitality and business community in Mayiladuthurai since 1989. Over the years we have served thousands of visitors through our lodge and other businesses.

VRS Homestay represents the next generation of our journey. Our goal is simple — to create a space where guests feel comfort, peace, and warmth just like home while exploring this spiritual town.

— VRS Family
