Our Story

A Legacy of Hospitality

A Home Away
From Home

VRS Homestay is more than just a place to stay — it is the natural evolution of a family deeply rooted in hospitality. Our journey began in 1989, when the VRS family established their lodge complex and other businesses in the heart of Mayiladuthurai, building decades of trust with guests and the local community.

Today, the second generation of the VRS family proudly carries that legacy forward — bringing a fresh vision of modern luxury to the same streets where their parents built their name. VRS Homestay was born from a deep understanding of what guests truly need: comfort, privacy, elegance, and a feeling of belonging.

"From a lodge to a luxury homestay — the same warmth, a new generation."

Prime Location

In the Heart of
Mayiladuthurai

VRS Homestay enjoys one of the most coveted addresses in Mayiladuthurai — steps away from the town's most iconic landmarks, surrounded by shopping, dining and essential services. Everything you need is right at your doorstep.

Near the Clock Tower

Our homestay is located right next to the famous Clock Tower — the main landmark and central point of Mayiladuthurai, making navigation effortless for every guest.

100m from Mayuranathar Temple

VRS Homestay is just 100 metres from the revered Mayuranathar Temple — one of Tamil Nadu's most sacred and historically significant Shiva temples. A blessing for every stay.

Surrounded by Shopping

Multiple shopping centres, local markets and retail stores surround the property — everything from daily essentials to clothing and souvenirs within walking distance.

Hospitals Nearby

Leading hospitals and medical facilities are located close by — ensuring peace of mind for families, senior travelers and guests with medical needs during their stay.
