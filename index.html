Ask Question

Common Inquiries

How do I manage my booking?
You can manage your booking by contacting us directly via phone or WhatsApp. Our concierge team is available 24/7 to assist with any changes or queries regarding your reservation.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 48 hours before check-in are fully refundable. After that, one night's charge may apply. Please contact us for special circumstances.
Are pets allowed in the residences?
At the moment, we are unable to accommodate pets in our homestay. We appreciate your understanding as we maintain a comfortable and hygienic environment for all our guests.
Do you offer railway station or bus stand transfers?
Currently, we do not provide railway station or bus stand pickup services. However, both locations are easily accessible from our homestay and local transportation options are readily available.
