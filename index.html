Common Inquiries

How do I manage my booking?
You can manage your booking by contacting us directly via phone or WhatsApp. Our concierge team is available 24/7 to assist with any changes or queries regarding your reservation.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 48 hours before check-in are fully refundable. After that, one night's charge may apply. Please contact us for special circumstances.
Are pets allowed in the residences?
We have a pet-friendly policy for selected residences. Please mention your pet during the booking process and we will do our best to accommodate you.
Do you offer airport transfers?
Yes, we offer premium airport transfer services on request. Please inform us of your flight details at least 24 hours in advance and we will arrange a comfortable pickup.
